## 🏆 Key Judge Q&A Defense Sheet

1. **"Why not just use ChatGPT or Claude directly?"**
   - *Answer:* Generic LLMs hallucinate entire answers, don't know the professor's specific syllabus or slide rules, and have no memory of the student. EduTrace AI performs line-by-line step diffing, grounds citations in actual lecture slides via vector RAG, and maintains a persistent Bayesian Beta-Binomial Learning Twin across the entire semester.

2. **"How does your Bayesian Learning Twin work mathematically?"**
   - *Answer:* We model student skill mastery as a conjugate $\text{Beta}(\alpha, \beta)$ probability distribution rather than a naive average. Every correct diagnostic or reassessment step performs a Bayesian conjugate update ($\alpha \to \alpha + \text{marks}, \beta \to \beta + \text{lost}$). This mathematically yields both expected mastery $\mathbb{E}[\theta] = \frac{\alpha}{\alpha + \beta}$ and a rigorous credible confidence interval that narrows as evidence accumulates.

3. **"How do you handle messy student handwriting?"**
   - *Answer:* In Stage 3 Forensics, ambiguous handwriting crops trigger our OCR uncertainty threshold ($conf < 0.80$). Instead of guessing, EduTrace surfaces a human-in-the-loop interactive verification pill for the student to confirm before diffing, guaranteeing 100% algebraic fidelity.
