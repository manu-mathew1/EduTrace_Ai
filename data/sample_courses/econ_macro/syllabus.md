## 🎯 Course Overview & Learning Objectives
ECON 202 develops rigorous theoretical and quantitative models of aggregate economic performance, economic growth, and business cycle fluctuations. Students will analyze macroeconomic equilibrium across goods, money, and labor markets using algebraic derivations, IS-LM diagrams, AS-AD frameworks, and policy simulations.

---

## 📊 Exam Structure & Topic Weightages

| Module | Core Skill / Macroeconomic Model | Exam Weight | Key Cognitive Traps & Pitfalls |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|
| **Module 1** | **The Keynesian Cross & Fiscal Multipliers** | **20%** | Omitting marginal income tax drag $t$ from the multiplier denominator; confusing autonomous vs induced expenditure shifts. |
| **Module 2** | **The IS-LM Framework & Financial Markets** | **25%** | Confusing shifts of the IS/LM curves vs movements along curves; neglecting the private investment crowding-out effect. |
| **Module 3** | **Aggregate Supply, Aggregate Demand (AS-AD) & Phillips Curve** | **25%** | Misidentifying supply vs demand shocks; assuming long-run monetary non-neutrality; stagflation policy trade-offs. |
| **Module 4** | **Neoclassical Growth (Solow-Swan Model)** | **15%** | Confusing levels of steady-state output per worker with long-run growth rate; capital depreciation miscalculations. |
| **Module 5** | **Open Economy Macroeconomics (Mundell-Fleming)** | **15%** | Flawed exchange rate regime assumptions; confusing fixed vs floating exchange rate monetary autonomy. |

---

## ⚖️ Grading & Partial Credit Rubric Policy
1. **Model Identification & Preconditions (30% of marks):** Correctly identifying the relevant market equilibrium (goods market vs money market vs labor market).
2. **Algebraic Derivation & Parameter Isolation (40% of marks):** Clear step-by-step differentiation of multiplier formulas and equilibrium conditions.
3. **Economic Intuition & Policy Interpretation (30% of marks):** Articulating the transmission mechanism (e.g., $G \uparrow \implies Y \uparrow \implies M_d \uparrow \implies r \uparrow \implies I \downarrow$).
