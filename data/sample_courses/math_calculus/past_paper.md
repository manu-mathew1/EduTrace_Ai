### Question 1 (10 Marks) — Advanced Integration
Evaluate the following indefinite integral showing all intermediate steps:
$$\int x \cos(2x) \, dx$$

* **Rubric Step 1 (2 Marks):** Correct substitution of parts using $u = x$ and $dv = \cos(2x) \, dx$.
* **Rubric Step 2 (3 Marks):** Accurate derivation of $du = dx$ and $v = \frac{1}{2}\sin(2x)$.
* **Rubric Step 3 (3 Marks):** Expansion of $uv - \int v \, du = \frac{1}{2}x\sin(2x) - \int \frac{1}{2}\sin(2x) \, dx$.
* **Rubric Step 4 (2 Marks):** Accurate integration of $-\frac{1}{2}\sin(2x)$ yielding $+\frac{1}{4}\cos(2x) + C$.

### Question 2 (10 Marks) — Differential Equations
Find the general solution to the first-order linear differential equation:
$$\frac{dy}{dx} + 2y = 4x$$

* **Rubric Step 1 (2 Marks):** Identify $P(x) = 2$ and $Q(x) = 4x$.
* **Rubric Step 2 (3 Marks):** Compute integrating factor $\mu(x) = e^{\int 2 \, dx} = e^{2x}$.
* **Rubric Step 3 (3 Marks):** Integrate $\int 4x e^{2x} \, dx$ using parts.
* **Rubric Step 4 (2 Marks):** Isolate $y(x) = 2x - 1 + C e^{-2x}$.
