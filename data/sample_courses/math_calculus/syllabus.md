# MATH 301: Multivariable Calculus & Differential Equations
**Term:** Fall Semester 2026 • **Department of Mathematics**  
**Instructor:** Prof. S. Kapoor • **Credits:** 4.0  

---

## 🎯 Course Overview & Learning Objectives
MATH 301 bridges single-variable differential calculus into multidimensional systems and physical mechanics. Students will master techniques for higher-order integration, linear ordinary differential equations, vector fields, and line integrals.

---

## 📊 Exam Structure & Topic Weightages

| Module | Core Skill / Topic | Exam Weight | Key Cognitive Traps |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|
| **Module 1** | **Advanced Integration Techniques** | **15%** | Bracket sign distribution in integration by parts; trigonometric substitution domain boundaries. |
| **Module 2** | **First-Order Linear Differential Equations** | **15%** | Integrating factor sign setup $\mu(x) = e^{\int P(x)dx}$; separation of variables confusion. |
| **Module 3** | **Multivariable Functions & Partial Derivatives** | **20%** | Chain rule for multivariable surfaces; directional gradient vectors. |
| **Module 4** | **Multiple Integrals (Double & Triple)** | **25%** | Jacobian transformations in cylindrical and spherical coordinates; boundary reversal. |
| **Module 5** | **Vector Calculus & Field Theorems** | **25%** | Divergence Theorem vs Stokes' Theorem circulation; line integrals in non-conservative fields. |

---

## ⚖️ Grading & Partial Credit Policy
1. **Method Selection (40% of marks per step):** Correct identification of technique (e.g. Parts vs Substitution).
2. **Algebraic Rigor (40% of marks per step):** Accurate distribution of negative signs, constants of integration $+C$, and boundary substitutions.
3. **Final Computation (20% of marks):** Simplified exact value without decimal approximations.
